AUC Aquavaders · MATE ROV Competition

Underwater robot (ROV)

An underwater robot built by students for the MATE competition. I joined the mechanical team in 2025 and have led it since June 2026.

Role Mechanical Team LeadSince June 2025Tools SolidWorks, ANSYS CFD and FEA

What the mechanical team does

  • Propulsion: where the thrusters go and how they point, so the robot can move and turn in every direction.
  • Buoyancy and stability: placing the components so the robot floats level and stays steady.
  • Structure: the frame, designed in CAD and checked with FEA; CFD to cut drag.
  • Fabrication, 3D printing and pool testing.

My role

In my first year I designed the propulsion and buoyancy layout and ran the CFD. As team lead, I split the work, manage the timeline, and coordinate with the software and electronics teams, so that the mechanics, wiring and controls fit together.
