Personal projects · Fusion 360 · 2024–2025

Personal CAD: F1 car, V10, GE90

I wanted to learn CAD, and it is easier to keep going when you are building something you enjoy. So I taught myself Fusion 360 on an F1 car, and it went from there: an engine for it, and then a jet engine.

Hours 50+ on the car aloneTool Fusion 360Self-taught yes

Formula 1 car

January–May 2024

Why: I wanted to learn CAD properly, and an F1 car was the obvious thing to learn it on, since it is what I enjoy most.

What is modelled: the whole car, from scratch, over 20 versions and 50+ hours: diffusers, Venturi tunnels, multi-element wings and suspension.

F1 V10 hybrid engine

July–November 2024

Why: I was disappointed by the 2026 engine rules, so I drew the engine I would have liked instead, as a concept.

What is modelled: a naturally aspirated V10 with an MGU-K hybrid motor, over 1,000 components, aimed at about 20,000 RPM and about 950 hp. Joints in Fusion 360 simulate the engine's motion.

GE90 jet engine

December 2024–January 2025

Why: I'm a bit of an aviation geek, and the GE90 is just a really cool engine.

What is modelled: the Boeing 777's engine, from fan to nozzle: the multi-stage compressor and turbine, the bypass ratio and the combustor.
